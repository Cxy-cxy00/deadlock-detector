Deadlock basics

4 Coffman conditions

01

Mutual exclusion

Only one thread can hold a lock.

02

Hold and wait

Each thread holds one account. It wants the other.

03

No preemption

Nobody can take a lock away.

04

Circular wait

Thread 1 waits for Thread 2. Thread 2 waits for Thread 1.

All 4 true → deadlock. Break one → no deadlock.
Speaker A · นายภูตะวัน ทรัพย์เกิด · 673030088403 / 16
[Speaker A — นายภูตะวัน ทรัพย์เกิด · 0:28 · 51 words] A deadlock needs four conditions. First, mutual exclusion: one thread per lock. Second, hold and wait: each holds one, wants another. Third, no preemption: nobody can take a lock away. Fourth, circular wait: they wait in a loop. Remove one, and there is no deadlock. Our tool looks for the loop.

Limits & early warning

What it can and cannot do

LIMIT

Linux, normal mutex

Linux programs only. No condition variables, rwlock or semaphore. No livelock.

LIMIT

Finds it after

A deadlock is found after it happens. It does not stop it.

ALSO

Warns early

Two locks taken in opposite orders → warning before a deadlock.
